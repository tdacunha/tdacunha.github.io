------
I am a 5th and final year Physics PhD candidate at Stanford University in the [Kavli Institute for Particle Astrophysics & Cosmology (KIPAC)](https://kipac.stanford.edu/).
I am advised by [Risa Wechsler](https://www.risawechsler.com/) and part of her [Galaxy Formation & Cosmology group](https://gfc.stanford.edu/).
My research interests are centered on constraining dark matter through near field astrophysics probes. I primarily work on flexible modeling techniques that can leverage dark-matter-only cosmological zoom-in simulations to generate realizations of dark matter subhalo, dwarf galaxy, dwarf galaxy stream, and stellar halo populations marginalizing over the large theoretical uncertainties. My goal is to enable and perform dark matter inference with existing and upcoming near field surveys by striking a balance between realistic and flexible forward forward modeling.

I grew up in Philadelphia, PA, and received my B.A. and concurrent M.S. in Physics (with a minor in Linguistics) from the University of Pennsylvania in 2022. 
Outside of research, I am an artist and love acrylic & watercolor painting as well as making digital astrophysics illustrations for KIPAC and animal cartoons. I also enjoy bouldering (inside) and reading (outside).
